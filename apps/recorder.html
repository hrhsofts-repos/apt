About

Record your voice, the microphone or the sound playing on the computer as Opus, MP3, AAC, FLAC or WAV. Recorder is part of HRHsofts Apps and is installed for every account on this computer, like the built-in applications.

  • Microphone, system audio or both mixed, from any input device
  • Pause and resume, with the paused time left out of the file
  • Noise reduction and voice levelling for microphones
  • Automatic stop after a chosen time, and before the disk fills up
  • Playback at 0.75x to 2x without changing the pitch
  • Rename, export to another format, trim silence, move to the trash with undo

Open HRHsofts Setup to add or remove it.

Install

From the terminal (adds the signed HRHsuite repository so Recorder keeps itself updated):

wget -qO- https://hrhsofts-repos.github.io/apt/install.sh | sudo sh -s -- recorder

If the repository is already set up, or on HRHsofts Ubuntu:

sudo apt install hrhsofts-recorder

Or download the .deb and install it with App Center or sudo apt install ./hrhsofts-recorder_1.1.2-1_all.deb.

What's new

Version 1.1.2 · 09 Nov 2026
  • Check for Updates in the main menu: new versions are downloaded from the HRHsuite update service and installed after one password prompt
  • A notification offers new versions automatically, at most once a day
  • Fix: the app could close with a memory error while listing microphones and cameras
Version 1.1.1 · 08 Nov 2026
  • The computer no longer suspends in the middle of a recording
  • Recordings reach the disk as they are made, so a crash or power cut loses almost nothing
  • A disk error while saving is reported instead of "Saved"; WAV recordings stop and save before the 4 GB WAV limit
  • Export and Trim Silence work for long recordings and for AAC (M4A) files, keep the recording's date and bit rate
  • Input gain can be changed while recording
  • Keys no longer act on the recording behind an open dialog; Undo after Move to Trash always works
  • Recordings carry their title and date as tags; Copy File and drag and drop into other apps
  • Left and Right seek 5 seconds while playing; recorder --stop and --pause for scripts and shortcuts
  • A notification when a recording stops by itself while the window is in the background
Version 1.1.0 · 30 Oct 2026
  • Microphone records the microphone even when Ubuntu has no default input chosen (it used to record silence)
  • Stereo recordings are really stereo; Mono halves the file size for voice
  • Reduce background noise and Even out voice volume clean up the microphone
  • Stop automatically after a chosen time, with a countdown while recording
  • A warning with a Sound Settings button when no sound comes from the microphone
  • Plugging in or removing a microphone updates the input list straight away
  • Playback speed from 0.75x to 2x without changing the pitch
  • Trim Silence for any recording (Ctrl+T)
